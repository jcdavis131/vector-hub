dumbmodel.com · methodology notebook

The machinery behind the hoops map, explained honestly.

We train neural networks that turn NBA player-seasons into 64-number vectors — positions on a map of how players play. This site is the notebook for those models: what they take in, what the numbers mean, what they cannot see, and where every headline number comes from. It is an explanation site, not a lab and not a storefront.

Plate 01 · the whole site in six lines

  • 12,966 NBA player-seasons, each mapped to 64 numbers by the MTNN v5 champion — the single model that serves everything, incumbent since 2026-07-25. Model card →
  • The map measures how players play, not how good they are. Style similarity is not value similarity. Embeddings →
  • Era twins = the true nearest neighbor from another decade in the served 64-d space. 1,313 players, recomputed 2026-10-12. Method →
  • Forecasts are made in embedding space — a held-out macro-MAE of 0.4382 is a distance between style vectors, not points per game. Forecasting →
  • Real data only, never synthetic. Fourteen box-score stats per player-season; no lineup data, no matchup context. Sources →
  • Every number traces to a source file or commit — and the ledger also lists what we don't know. Provenance →
