data · sources & pipeline

Real data only. Never synthetic.

Everything the hoops models learn comes from real recorded games. No generated players, no simulated seasons, no filler. What follows is every source we claim — enumerated from the pipeline, not invented — plus the gaps we know about.

Plate 02 · what is not a source

This repo's history contains files that fed the previous hub site — Vegas lines, daily boards, feed flags (assets/data/vegas_*.json, boards_2026_08_*.json). None of that trained the hoops model. Some of those pipeline logs even document honestly-synthetic deterministic data for the old games' lines. The hoops embeddings come from the hoops training corpus above, full stop. If a file isn't in the sources table, it didn't train the model.

source: this repo's assets/data/data_pipeline_log.md (boards / Vegas backfill / feed pipeline sections) vs. the hoops corpus — enumerated during build, no invented sources.

Plate 03 · known gaps

  • Stat scope. 14 per-100 box-score stats; no lineup data, no matchup context, defense via steals/blocks only. (See /embeddings.)
  • Era data-quality regimes. Pre-2000s tracking data is thinner — older seasons' vectors rest on less recorded detail. Era comparisons span these regimes; read them accordingly.
  • Minutes field. The mpg field was found uncorrelated with real minutes and was dropped from forecasting inputs; real 2025-26 minutes used instead.
  • No synthetic backfill. Where history is thin, we leave it thin rather than generate plausible filler. Gaps are visible; that is the policy.

sources: stat scope per plan §6b; era regimes and mpg flag per plan §6a and project records.
