glossary · plain words

The words, without the fog.

Every term this site uses, defined the way you'd explain it at a whiteboard.

embedding
A player-season turned into a list of numbers (here, 64) that places it in a space where nearby means "similar." The model's output, not its input.
dimension (d)
One of the numbers in the vector. "64-d" means 64 numbers per player-season. The dimensions aren't named things — together they form a position.
cosine similarity
A score for how alike two vectors' directions are — the angle between two arrows. Near 1: very similar style. Near 0: unrelated. It ignores overall volume and hears the shape of the game.
PCA (principal component analysis)
A classic technique for squashing many numbers into fewer while keeping as much variation as possible. The atlas's x/y/z is a PCA(3) of the 14 stats keeping 62.4% of their variance — a view, not the model.
tower
One small network inside the MTNN, reading one family of stats. The towers' outputs are concatenated and fused into the final 64-d vector.
fusion
The step where the towers' outputs are combined (here, concatenated, then run through an MLP) into the single 64-d embedding.
champion / challenger
The champion is the one model serving everything. A challenger is a new model trained offline that must beat the champion on held-out data to take its place. See /methodology.
held-out
Data the model never saw in training, reserved for honest testing. A win on held-out data is the load-bearing promotion gate.
era twin (argmax)
A player's nearest neighbor from another decade in the served 64-d space — the "argmax" (highest-similarity pick) with the era constraint. Recomputed for all 1,313 players on 2026-10-12.
macro-MAE
Mean absolute error, averaged evenly across groups (here, across players) rather than across all predictions at once — so a few high-volume players can't dominate the score. Arc Forecaster v1's is 0.4382 in embedding space.
z-score (within season)
Rescaling each stat by its season's mean and spread, so a number means "how unusual was this, that year." Lets a 1962 season and a 2024 season speak the same language.
per-100 stats
Box-score stats scaled to 100 possessions, so players with different minutes are comparable on rate, not just totals.
calibration (ECE)
Whether a model's reported confidence means what it says. ECE (expected calibration error) under 0.05 is one of the promotion gates.
CKA
Centered kernel alignment — a check that a challenger model's internal representations still resemble the champion's, so a swap doesn't silently redraw the map.
drift
Numbers on the site quietly disagreeing with the served model — stale copy, wrong dimensions, old counts. This site's anti-drift showcase is /provenance.
provenance
The record of where a number came from — file, commit, or measurement. Every headline number on this site has one.
single-model serving
The policy that exactly one champion model answers every query — no shadow models, no split traffic. The map you see and the map we evaluate are the same map.
style map vs value map
The 64-d space measures how players play, not how good they are. Nearby = similar style. It is not a ranking, and has no "up."
